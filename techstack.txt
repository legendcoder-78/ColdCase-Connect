Here is the best, most practical tech stack for this hackathon project, kept short and clearly explaining what each tool actually does:

**Frontend (User Interface)**

* **Streamlit (Python):** The fastest way to build your web dashboard. It lets you create file uploaders, score sliders, and side-by-side image comparisons in minutes without writing complex web code (HTML/CSS).

**Backend (The Logic)**

* **FastAPI (Python):** Connects your dashboard to the AI models and database. It is extremely fast, lightweight, and the industry standard for handling machine learning tasks.

**AI Models (The Brains)**

* **OpenAI CLIP:** Analyzes the crime scene photos and turns them into searchable data. This handles the "Visual Similarity" score.
* **Sentence-BERT (SBERT):** Reads the written case descriptions and understands the underlying meaning, not just the keywords. This handles the "Text Similarity" score.
* **Gemini API (or OpenAI API):** The text-generator. It reads the matched cases and writes the plain-English explanation of why they are connected.

**Database (The Memory)**

* **ChromaDB:** A "vector database." Standard databases (like SQL) cannot search by image or meaning. ChromaDB is built specifically to store and instantly search the AI data generated by CLIP and SBERT. It runs locally, meaning zero setup time and zero lag during your demo.

**Programming Language**

* **Python:** Everything above runs perfectly in Python, meaning your team only needs to use one language for the entire project.
